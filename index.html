AntSort Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

AntSort is provided by LIZUOJUN. This policy explains how the current App handles information.

1. Data on your device

AntSort stores game progress, coins, reward records, daily boost usage and preferences on your device. A game-specific Game Center player ID and the highest successfully submitted score are also stored locally to avoid duplicate submissions. System language, accessibility preferences and power/thermal state are used locally to adjust the display. We use standard iOS storage protections and do not operate a game-save server.

2. Apple Game Center

When the home screen appears, the App checks your Game Center sign-in status. If signed in, your highest consecutively cleared level, starting from level 1, may be submitted to Apple automatically, even without opening the leaderboard. Apple handles authentication and may display your nickname, avatar and score to other players. We do not receive your Apple Account password.

You can play without Game Center or sign out through Apple’s settings. Game Center data is handled under Apple’s Game Center privacy information.

3. Retention and deletion

Local data remains while the App’s data is on your device. Deleting the App removes its local data; offloading it preserves that data. System backups may retain or restore it. Deleting the App or signing out does not delete Apple’s leaderboard records; use Apple’s privacy and account controls for data it holds.

4. Support and privacy requests

If you email us, your address, message and attachments are processed through our email provider to respond to your request. We retain correspondence only as reasonably needed for the request and legal obligations. Contact us for applicable access, correction or deletion requests. We cannot remotely access device-only saves; Apple manages its own data.

5. Children and policy updates

We do not request children’s names or contact details during gameplay. Parents can manage Game Center through Apple’s parental controls and contact us with privacy concerns.

Policy changes will be posted with an updated date. This page has no tracking scripts; a website host may process necessary access logs.
