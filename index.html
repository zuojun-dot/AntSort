1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AntSort (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by [DEVELOPER_OR_COMPANY_NAME] (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It covers the current version of the App and privacy-related communications with us.

Privacy contact: [PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL].

You can play the core puzzles without creating an AntSort account. Game Center is an Apple service used for the leaderboard and is separate from an AntSort account.

2. Information used on your device

The App stores the following information locally to keep the game working between sessions:

  • Your current level and completed levels.
  • Your in-game coin balance and reward records used to prevent duplicate crediting.
  • The date and amount of your daily speed-boost allowance already used.
  • Your home-animation and haptic-feedback preferences.
  • A Game Center identifier scoped to this game, together with the highest score successfully submitted for that player. This local receipt prevents unnecessary duplicate submissions.

The App also reads your system language, Reduce Motion preference, low-power mode and thermal state on the device to adapt text, animation and rendering. These system states are not sent by the App as analytics.

We do not operate a server that receives your local game saves, coin balance or settings. The current App does not provide its own cloud-save synchronization.

3. Apple Game Center

The App initializes Game Center when the home screen first appears to check whether you are signed in. If you are already signed in, leaderboard scores may be submitted automatically in the background; opening the leaderboard is not required for score submission. A sign-in screen, when needed, is shown after you request the leaderboard.

The submitted score is your highest consecutively cleared level, starting from level 1. The App sends this score to Apple through GameKit for the configured leaderboard and current Game Center player. It may retry a pending score after a later completion, sign-in, return to the foreground or explicit retry.

Apple associates the score with your Game Center account. Your Game Center nickname, avatar and score can be visible to other Game Center users. The App displays rankings through Apple’s native interface and does not independently request or store your nickname, avatar or friend list.

Apple handles Game Center authentication; we do not receive your Apple Account password. Apple also processes Game Center activity under its own policies. See Game Center & Privacy and Apple’s Privacy Policy for Apple’s data practices, sharing controls and privacy choices.

4. Permissions, advertising and tracking

The current App does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not request push-notification permission.

The App contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or independent crash-reporting SDK. We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and we do not sell your information.

The current App has no in-app purchase or subscription system. In-game coins are gameplay rewards; the App does not collect payment information. Apple’s App Store, operating-system diagnostics and Game Center are governed by Apple’s own settings and policies.

5. Information you choose to send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your question, investigate a problem or respond to a privacy request. It is handled through the email service used for that correspondence, not through an in-game data-collection system.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

6. How information is shared

Game Center scores are sent to Apple as described above. Your local game saves and settings are not uploaded to a developer-operated game server. We do not send them to advertising networks or data brokers.

Information you send us for support may be processed by the email provider needed to deliver and manage the correspondence. We may disclose that correspondence when necessary to comply with a valid legal obligation or protect legal rights, limited to what is reasonably necessary.

Apple’s services may process information in the countries where Apple and its service providers operate. Apple’s policies explain its handling of that information.

7. Retention, deletion and backups

Local game information is retained while the App’s data remains on your device. Deleting the App removes its local data from that device. Using iOS “Offload App” instead preserves documents and data. Device backups or transfers may contain or restore game data according to your Apple and device settings; this is separate from an AntSort cloud-save service.

Deleting the App or signing out of Game Center does not automatically delete scores already held by Apple. The App does not include an in-app deletion control for Game Center records. Use Apple’s privacy and account controls or contact Apple about data it holds.

We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

For more information, see Apple’s guidance on iCloud backups and deleting or offloading apps.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can play the core game without signing in to Game Center. Manage or sign out of Game Center through Apple’s settings if you do not want to participate in its online features. You can change the App’s animation and haptic preferences in its settings and manage app backups through your device settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing depends on it, or raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

Contact us about information you have provided directly to us. We do not have remote access to your device-only game saves. For information Apple holds independently, use Apple’s privacy channels. We will help explain the available steps within our control.

9. Children’s information

The App does not ask players to enter a birth date, a real name or contact information during gameplay. It does not include behavioral advertising or a developer-operated player account.

Parents and guardians can manage Game Center through Apple’s account and parental controls. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate steps.

10. Security

The App uses standard iOS application storage and Apple-provided GameKit interfaces. Local data is kept within the App’s storage area under the platform’s protections. Please protect your device and Apple Account credentials. No system can guarantee absolute security.

11. This policy page

This HTML page includes no analytics scripts, advertising tags, tracking cookies or third-party fonts. If it is published on a website, the hosting provider may process network information needed to serve the page, such as IP addresses and access logs, under its own privacy practices. That website hosting is separate from the App’s local game storage.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The revised page will show a new “Last updated” date. If applicable law requires additional notice or permission for a change, we will provide it before that change takes effect.
